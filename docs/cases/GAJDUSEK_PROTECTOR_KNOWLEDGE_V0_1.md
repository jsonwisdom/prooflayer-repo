# Gajdusek Protector-Knowledge Case Discovery v0.1

**State:** DISCOVERY / NON-CANON / RECEIPTS-FIRST  
**Wrapper:** BoxDee  
**Subject:** D. Carleton Gajdusek  
**Purpose:** Track institutional knowledge, enabling infrastructure, intervention failures, and post-conviction support without converting association into accusation.

## Boundary rule

A named person or institution is **not** a "protector" merely because they knew Gajdusek, worked with him, supported his science, or appeared in his journals. Promotion to a protection/facilitation claim requires a dated receipt showing (1) knowledge of abuse or a material warning signal and (2) an action or omission materially connected to continued access, concealment, interference, or avoidance of consequence.

Statuses: `PROVEN | OBSERVED | CLAIMED | HOLD | CONFLICT | REJECTED`.

## Proven anchor

Gajdusek pleaded guilty in February 1997 to two counts of child abuse. The Washington Post reported that, when the judge asked whether he was pleading guilty because he was in fact guilty, he answered yes. This anchors the case in adjudicated/admitted conduct rather than rumor.

## High-value receipts

### NIH infrastructure — OBSERVED
Rachel Aviv reports that Gajdusek used HHS letterhead for guardianship declarations and had lab colleagues witness them; NIH staff typed journals, NIH funds printed them, and at least one journal discussing pederasty/pedophilia was distributed to hundreds of colleagues.

Source: https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2026/08/24/the-scientist-who-lived-with-dozens-of-children-from-the-south-pacific

### Warning signal: Judith Farquhar — OBSERVED / NOT A PROTECTOR FINDING
Aviv reports that Farquhar warned Gajdusek that personal journals should be printed privately because passages could create suspicions of sexual misconduct. This is evidence of an early warning signal, not evidence that Farquhar protected him.

### Warning signal / intervention: Mark Godec — OBSERVED / WHISTLEBLOWER
Aviv reports that Godec read the journals, raised concerns with colleagues, and in 1995 brought annotated journals to Senator Nancy Kassebaum; OIG and FBI investigations followed. This node belongs on the intervention rail, not the protector rail.

### NIH/NINDS management knowledge — CLAIMED / HOLD
Aviv reports that David Asher told an OIG agent that whatever Gajdusek had done was done openly and with the full knowledge and consent of NINDS management. This is a attributed statement, not yet a primary-document finding establishing exactly who knew what and when.

**Next receipt:** OIG interview memoranda, NIH/NINDS management correspondence, personnel records, Inspector General reports, congressional records, and contemporaneous journal distribution lists.

### Earlier Maryland investigation — PROVEN THAT INVESTIGATION OCCURRED / HOLD ON NIH KNOWLEDGE
The Washington Post reported that Montgomery County police investigated Gajdusek in 1989 after information concerning adults allegedly abused as children, but the investigation was suspended when no current victims could be identified. Whether NIH management knew of that investigation at the time remains a separate question.

Source: https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1996/04/05/nih-scientist-charged-with-abusing-teen/867aac5f-d87a-49a4-af3f-d70d9795561a/

### Criminal-case interference signal — REPORTED BY PROSECUTOR / IDENTITY HOLD
The Washington Post reported prosecutor Scott Rolle saying that potential witnesses became unavailable and that an unnamed Gajdusek supporter threatened the principal victim and offered airline tickets to return to Micronesia. The conduct is a high-priority discovery target; the identity and documentary receipts must be independently established before naming anyone as responsible.

Source: https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1997/02/19/nobel-winner-guilty-of-abusing-boy/b1a351e6-49dd-428b-aa7d-9b81077921bd/

### NIH employment consequence — OBSERVED
Aviv reports that NIH did not fire Gajdusek; he was allowed to resign. Determine the decision chain, officials involved, applicable rules, and whether the outcome reflected ordinary personnel procedure, negotiated disposition, or discretionary protection.

### Post-conviction/post-release support — OBSERVED, NOT AUTOMATICALLY PROTECTION
Aviv reports multiple forms of support after the guilty plea or incarceration, including efforts to provide academic offices, appointments, practical assistance, attendance at his release, and a French research center providing an office and publishing his jail-year journal. These are support relationships. They become "protection" only if receipts show knowledge plus material facilitation of evasion, concealment, renewed access to minors, obstruction, or similar conduct.

## Subject's own protector statement — PROVEN AS A STATEMENT / NOT PROOF OF IDENTITIES
Aviv reports that two months before his 2008 death Gajdusek wrote that he had carried his "lifelong pederasty" into his medical career and that "Without protectors I would never have succeeded." This proves that Gajdusek himself asserted he had protectors. It does **not** by itself prove who they were or what they did.

## Promotion test

A candidate may move from `HOLD` only when the record can answer:

1. **WHO** is the exact person/institution?
2. **WHAT** did they know — rumor, journal language, allegation, admission, police inquiry, or conviction?
3. **WHEN** did they know it?
4. **SOURCE** — what contemporaneous receipt proves that knowledge?
5. **ACTION** — what did they do or fail to do afterward?
6. **CAUSATION** — did that action materially preserve access, status, mobility, resources, silence, or avoidance of consequence?
7. **ALTERNATIVE** — is there a non-protective explanation consistent with the receipts?

Until all seven survive replay: `PROTECTOR = HOLD`.
